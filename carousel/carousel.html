HACK #1

Give it a role

AI answers like whoever you tell it to be.

✕ WEAK

Explain Ohm's law.

✓ STRONG

Act as a friendly physics teacher. Explain Ohm's law to a first-year student with one real-life example.

💡
A role sets the tone, depth and vocabulary of the answer.

HACK #2

Add context

Tell it who you are and what you need it for.

✕ WEAK

Make a study plan.

✓ STRONG

Make a study plan. I'm a B.Tech student, exam in 10 days, 3 subjects, I can study 4 hours a day, weak in Maths.

🎯
No context = generic answer. More details = a plan made for you.

HACK #3

Ask for a format

Decide how the answer should look before you get it.

✕ WEAK

Compare Python and Java.

✓ STRONG

Compare Python and Java in a table with 5 rows: speed, syntax, jobs, uses, learning curve.

📊
Try: table · bullet points · 5 lines · step-by-step · flashcards

HACK #4

Show an example

One example beats a paragraph of instructions.

✕ WEAK

Write Instagram captions for my tech page.

✓ STRONG

Write 5 captions in this style: "This free tool does in 10 sec what took me 2 hours 🤯 Save it."

🧩
AI copies the style, length and energy of your example.

HACK #5

Let it ask you first

Not sure what details to give? Make AI interview you.

✓ ADD THIS LINE

Before you answer, ask me 3 questions so you understand exactly what I need.

AI REPLIES

1. What's the deadline?
2. Who is it for?
3. Short or detailed?

🤝
Works great for resumes, projects and plans.

HACK #6

Make it check itself

AI can make mistakes. Ask it to review its own answer.

✓ FOLLOW-UP PROMPT

Review your answer. Find any mistakes or weak points, then give me an improved version.

✓ FOR FACTS

Which parts of this should I double-check from a trusted source?

🔍
Always verify facts, numbers and code before you use them.
