Skill #1

The 5-part prompt formula

1Role: "Act as a senior marketer…"
2Task: say exactly what you want done
3Context: audience, goal, background
4Format: table, bullets, 100 words…
5Constraints: tone, dos & don'ts, examples
Vague in = vague out
Tool · Claude

Use Claude for
deep work

Best for

  • Writing, editing & a natural tone
  • Coding & debugging (Claude Code)
  • Long PDFs, contracts & reports
Pro tip: wrap your inputs in tags.
"Summarise <report>…</report> in 5 bullets for a CEO."

Tool · ChatGPT

Use ChatGPT for
creative firepower

Best for

  • Brainstorming & content ideas
  • Image generation & editing
  • Voice chats & custom GPTs
Pro tip: ask for options, then refine.
"Give 10 hooks for a reel on X. Rate each 1–10 & improve the top 3."

Tool · Gemini

Use Gemini for
research & Google

Best for

  • Live web research (Deep Research)
  • Gmail, Docs, Sheets & Drive
  • YouTube & video understanding
Pro tip: point it at your sources.
"Using @Gmail, list every deadline from this week as a table."
