FAQ

Questions students ask

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything runs inside your browser, so there is no server cost and no login. Smarter AI summaries will come later as an optional upgrade.

How are summaries made? Is it AI?

The summariser picks the most important sentences in your notes and organises them into points, definitions, formulas and likely exam questions. It does not invent anything, so it never goes outside your syllabus. Always read through it once before your exam.

Are my notes uploaded anywhere?

No. Files are read on your own device. Only the free reading tools (for PDF, Word and photos) are downloaded the first time you use them.

Photo of my handwritten notes isn't read correctly

Photo reading works best on printed text or very neat handwriting, in good light, held flat. Check the text it finds and correct it before summarising.

Can I submit the handwritten pages as my assignment?

They are made for your own revision. Many colleges require assignments in your own handwriting, so check your college rules.

Which subjects work best?

Theory subjects with definitions, types, advantages and steps (OS, DBMS, CN, DSA theory, EDC, Machines, Surveying…). Long derivations and numericals are kept as-is in the summary.
