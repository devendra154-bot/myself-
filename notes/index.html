Smart AI (optional)

Free offline tools work without this. Turn on Smart AI to get simple-English rewrites, Telugu explanations and AI exam answers for your own notes. It uses your own free Google Gemini key, so DevInventor pays nothing and your key stays on this device.

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with Google.
  2. Click Create API key and copy it.
  3. Paste it below and switch Smart AI on.

🔒 When Smart AI is on, the notes you send to an AI button go to Google Gemini. Everything else stays on your device.
