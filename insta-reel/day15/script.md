## Recording tips
- Quiet room, phone ~20 cm from your mouth, no fan/AC noise
- Pause ~0.5 sec between lines (I'll tighten long gaps)
- Read in order, one take is fine. Same friendly, confident energy as yesterday
- Send as mp3 / m4a / wav
