## The STUDY prompt pack (to send people who comment STUDY)

**1. Explain simply**
```
Here are my notes on [topic]. Explain them in simple words, like you're teaching a 10-year-old, with one real-life example. Then give me the 5 most important points.
[paste notes]
```

**2. Flashcards**
```
Make 15 flashcards from these notes. Format: Q: ... / A: ... Keep answers short.
[paste notes]
```

**3. Quiz me**
```
Quiz me on [topic], one question at a time, with A/B/C/D options. Wait for my answer. If I'm wrong, explain why and give the correct answer. After 10 questions, give my score and the topics I should revise.
```

Tip: AI can make mistakes — always double-check facts, formulas and dates with your textbook. If anything is unclear, tell Claude: "doubts unte MCQs lo adugu".
