## Setup guide (to send people who comment AUTO)

1. Instagram → Settings → Account type → switch to **Professional** (Creator or Business).
2. Go to **manychat.com** → sign up → connect your Instagram (it may ask to link a Facebook Page — follow the steps).
3. **Automation → New** → trigger: **"User comments on your post or reel"**.
4. Select the reel → add the **keyword** (e.g. `PROMPT`).
5. Action 1: **public comment reply** — "DM chesa, check cheyyandi! 📩"
6. Action 2: **DM message** — your prompt or link.
7. Test from another account → then **Set Live**.

Note: ManyChat has a free plan (with limits). Menu names can change — check the latest steps in the app.
