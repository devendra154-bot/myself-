## Recording tips
- Quiet room, phone ~20 cm from your mouth, no fan/AC noise
- Pause ~0.5 sec between lines (I'll tighten long gaps)
- At **⏸ PAUSE 3 sec** marks, stay silent — I insert the old voice clips there
- Same energy as yesterday: friendly, confident, slightly fast
- Send as mp3 / m4a / wav
