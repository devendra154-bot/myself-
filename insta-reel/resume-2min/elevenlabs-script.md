## The prompt (to send people who comment RESUME)

```
You are an expert resume writer. Write an ATS-friendly, one-page resume for me for the job below.

My details:
[education, skills, projects, internships, achievements]

Job description:
[paste the full job description]

Rules:
- Use the important keywords from the job description naturally.
- Write achievements with numbers where I gave them (e.g. "increased X by 20%").
- Simple format: clear headings, bullet points, no tables, no photos, no columns.
- One page only.
- Do NOT invent any experience, skills or numbers — only use what I gave you.
If anything is missing or unclear, ask me in MCQs before writing.
```
