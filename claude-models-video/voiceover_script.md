## Recording tips
- Use a quiet room and hold the phone 15–20 cm away. Don't add music; I add the music and SFX.
- Pause briefly at full stops and commas. The subtitles change at those pauses.
- If you change any words, send me the final text so the subtitles match.
- A single file with all 7 lines also works. Leave about 1 second of silence between lines.
